Reproducing it without that copy
--------------------------------
The catalog is fully reproducible from material in this repository plus
the raw JMA archives:

    python3 build_jma_catalog.py \
        --zips data/jma_catalog \
        --region data/csep_japan/japan.forecast.nodes \
        --out data/jma_catalog \
        --working-catalog --min-magnitude 1.0

  - builder:  build_jma_catalog.py v1.1.0, committed
  - region:   data/csep_japan/japan.forecast.nodes, committed, sha256
              94f94b19a0b1eaf1db125b0570005c4724b526b0aaf38e91d7482704fe50dcf2
  - raw data: the eight JMA Seismological Bulletin hypocenter archives,
              sha256 recorded at commit 0d5a9ea in sha256_raw.txt, freely
              downloadable from JMA

The regenerated file must hash to the sha256 above. The committed
manifest, attrition report and attrition JSON record the full result and
are sufficient to audit the filtering without the catalog itself.

This follows the pattern already used for the raw JMA archives, which are
gitignored with their hashes committed in sha256_raw.txt.
